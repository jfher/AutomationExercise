# Contact Module Analysis

## Module

Contact

---

## Objective

Allow users to contact customer support through the Contact Us form.

---

## Functionalities

- Fill Form
- Attach File to Form
- Submit Form

---

## Entry Points

- Home Page

---

## Exit Points

- Contact us Page
- Home Page

---

## Functional Requirements

### FR-CONTACT-001

Users can access the Contact Us page.

### FR-CONTACT-002

Users can submit the contact form.

### FR-CONTACT-003

Users can upload a file.

### FR-CONTACT-004

Users must confirm the submission.

### FR-CONTACT-005

A success message is displayed after submission.

### FR-CONTACT-006

Required fields must be validated.
